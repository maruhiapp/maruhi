maruhi

You have been invited to a maruhi project

maruhi is end-to-end encrypted: accepting an invite declares your keys, and that happens only on your machine, in the maruhi CLI. There is no way to accept an invite in the browser.

Accept the invite

  1. Install the maruhi CLI if you don't have it yet.
  2. Copy the full invite link from your browser's address bar, including everything after #.
  3. Pass the link to the CLI as-is (keep the single quotes so your shell doesn't eat the # part): 
    maruhi invite accept '<paste the full invite link here>'
  4. The CLI walks you through the rest: verifying the inviter's key fingerprint and declaring your own keys.

This page does not — and cannot — read your invite link. The part after # never leaves your browser: it is not sent to the server, and this page contains no scripts.
